 LP BUILD SPECIFICATION
Executive implementation notes based on the selected visual direction.
Element
Direction
Brand hierarchy
Chez La Fête is primary. Serving Good Vibes appears as parent endorsement: 'Part of Serving Good Vibes'.
Visual language
Elegant French/Louisiana hospitality: ivory background, black typography, restrained gold accents, fleur-de-lis details.
Hero
Large real exterior/night image. Position the dual business model immediately: stays + private events.
Conversion
Two separate funnels: booking availability for stays and inquiry form for events.
Navigation
Stays; Private Events; Party Rentals; City Events; Sister Stays; Practical Info.
Content order
Stay first, Events second, then add-ons, city context, sister stays and practical information.
Photography
Use real property photography throughout; distinguish rooms, outdoor entertaining and event setups.
Cross-sell
Party Rentals links to Social Entertainment/SGV services; Sister Stays links to SGV portfolio.
