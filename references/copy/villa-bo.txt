Element
Direction
Brand
Villa BO is primary; Serving Good Vibes appears as a discreet parent endorsement.
Visual language
Premium urban hospitality. Warm off-white background, terracotta/rust accent, elegant serif display type, clean editorial grid.
Hero
Use the selected Villa BO building/exterior imagery only - no Chez La Fête imagery. Keep architecture prominent.
Booking
Availability module immediately below hero with check-in, check-out and guest fields.
Story image
Use the original supplied BO/family photograph without altering faces or recreating the people.
Content
Balance property sales arguments with neighborhood, lifestyle, medical tourism and practical stay information.
Practical info
Public-facing amenities only. Do not publish Wi-Fi passwords or other private access credentials.
Photography
Real Villa BO apartment, balcony, building and amenity photography. Avoid generic substitutions.
