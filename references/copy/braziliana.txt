 LP BUILD SPECIFICATION
Executive implementation notes based on the selected visual direction.
Element
Direction
Brand hierarchy
Braziliana is the philanthropic/community platform. Serving Good Vibes logo appears as the parent brand.
Naming
Always spell 'Braziliana' with Z. 'Sala Braziliana' must also use Z.
Visual language
Brazilian cultural collage: cream paper texture, deep green, yellow, blue and red accents; expressive illustration and bold display typography.
Hero
Culture-led visual with immediate Join Braziliana form. Form should remain prominent and conversion-oriented.
Navigation
Cultural Exchange; Sala Braziliana; Events; Food & Beverage; Braziliana House Band; Media & Experiences; Products & Merchandising; Sponsors.
Sala Braziliana
Dedicated feature block with logo, address, proposition and four core uses: live music, art exhibitions, private events, cultural gatherings.
Content rhythm
Alternate illustrated/photographic modules; strong horizontal color bands for Food & Beverage, House Band, Media and Merch.
Partners
Large partner-logo wall near the bottom to demonstrate ecosystem and community impact.
Final CTA
Repeat Join Braziliana at page end.
