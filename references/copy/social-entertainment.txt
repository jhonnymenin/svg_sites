 LP BUILD SPECIFICATION
Executive implementation notes based on the selected visual direction.
Element
Direction
Brand hierarchy
Social Entertainment is the parent/company brand. Serving Good Vibes appears as a major portfolio platform, not as the site masterbrand.
Visual language
Editorial collage / retro Americana / textured paper / black, cream, rust, mustard and muted teal. Strong condensed display typography.
Hero
Large left-aligned statement with architectural/hospitality collage on the right. Two primary CTAs.
Navigation
Food + Drink; Serving Good Vibes; Now + Next; Our People; SC Advising; Contact; Partner With Us.
Modules
Horizontal card rails for Food + Drink and Now + Next; people strip; SC Advising service bar; newsletter footer.
Photography
Real brand, venue, people and product imagery. Avoid generic stock imagery.
CTA behavior
Clear destination CTAs at section level; Partner With Us remains persistent in header.
