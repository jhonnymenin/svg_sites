# Art Direction

Before writing implementation code, establish a coherent visual concept.

Read:
- CLAUDE.md
- relevant files in /docs
- relevant files and imagery in /references

Then determine:

## Concept
Define one clear visual idea in a short sentence.

## Visual personality
Identify 3–6 qualities that should characterize the experience.

## Typography
Determine:
- display typography behavior
- body typography behavior
- hierarchy
- scale
- casing
- tracking
- alignment tendencies

## Composition
Determine:
- grid behavior
- symmetry or asymmetry
- density
- whitespace
- image-to-text relationship
- section rhythm

## Photography and imagery
Determine:
- preferred subjects
- crop behavior
- aspect ratios
- treatment
- overlays
- texture
- relationship with typography

## Color
Define how colors behave, not only which colors exist.

## Motion
Define a motion philosophy:
- entrance behavior
- scroll behavior
- hover behavior
- transitions
- intensity

## Signature device
Choose at least one recurring visual device that helps make the site recognizable.

## Anti-patterns
Explicitly identify visual conventions that would weaken this specific concept.

Document durable decisions in /docs/DESIGN.md when appropriate.

Do not begin full implementation until the visual direction is coherent.
